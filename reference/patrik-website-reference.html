OVERVIEW

A revolutionary shape concept that combines an extremely short and fast rocker line with a modern outline to create our most radical freestyle board ever.
More planing power, more speed, more pop. This board truely does everything better than the previous Airstyle generation.
The new 85 is the latest addition to the family for lighter riders and strong winds while the 92 is our PWA contest machine.
The 99 offers you early planing and amazing sliding and rotation abilities.

 

DETAILS

CONSTRUCTION

The lighter the board the easier the moves - but if the board breaks it is simply annoying to deal with repairs and losing sailing time.
Our pro riders chose a stronger construction over the light weight and we offer you what the Pro’s enjoy the most!!

Our full deck Carbon Kevlar is light but still impact strong and the Biax fibre on the bottom is giving the extra flex needed to absorb the uncountable landings.

About Us

With over 25 years of technical expertise, shaper and designer Patrik Diethelm is far more than just another name in windsurfing. From his beginnings as a shop customer to becoming a high-profile F2 designer and PWA professional, founding the PATRIK brand alongside multiple world champion Karin Jaggi in 2010 was a natural next step. Today, we design and produce premium windsurfing boards and sails, rig components, foils, fins, and some of the most advanced wings and boards in the industry.
Windsurfing is where we come from - and it’s still what drives us. Over the years, we’ve grown to love winging, surf foiling, and every other wind sport just as passionately.
